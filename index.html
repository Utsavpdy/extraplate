ExtraPlate
Rescue Surplus. Reduce Waste. Feed More.

Good food should feed people,
not fill a dustbin.

ExtraPlate is a digital bridge between surplus food and the people who can put it to good use. Hotels, banquet halls, caterers and event hosts post extra food — nearby NGOs discover it by distance and freshness, claim it, and complete the pickup with an OTP check.

1

Donate

Donors post surplus food with servings, packaging and a safe-time window.

→
2

Discover

NGOs find nearby listings on the Rescue Radar, filtered by distance zone and time left.

→
3

Rescue

A volunteer collects the food and the handover is confirmed with a private 4-digit OTP.
