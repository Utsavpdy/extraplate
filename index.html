ExtraPlate
Rescue Surplus. Reduce Waste. Feed More.

Good food should feed people,
not fill a dustbin.

ExtraPlate is a digital bridge between surplus food and the people who can put it to good use. It connects surplus food from weddings, banquets and hotels around Knowledge Park 2, Greater Noida with verified local NGOs — donors post extra food, NGOs discover it by distance and freshness, claim it, and complete the pickup with an OTP check.
